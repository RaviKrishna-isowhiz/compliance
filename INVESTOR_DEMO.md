# TBCP Compliance System - Investor Demo Guide

## Quick Demo Script (10-15 minutes)

### Setup
Open browser to: **http://localhost:3000**

---

## Demo Sequence

### Screen 1: Introduction (30 seconds)
Show the header and navigation.

**What to say:**
> "This is our federal compliance management system for the TBCP grant. We track compliance across 8 federal frameworks and manage all associated risks. Let me show you how it works."

---

### Screen 2: Dashboard Tab (5 minutes)
**Click: "Dashboard & Reports" tab**

#### A. Overview Tab (2 minutes)
Show the compliance score circle and quick stats.

**Key Points to Highlight:**

1. **Compliance Score Circle** (82/100)
   > "Our overall compliance score is 82 out of 100. This is a 'Good' standing. A score above 80 indicates strong institutional controls."

2. **Quick Stats**
   - "5 of our 8 frameworks are fully compliant"
   - "3 frameworks are in partial compliance - we're actively working on these"
   - "We have 3 critical issues that are being remediated"

3. **Risk Distribution**
   - Show the 4-box breakdown (3 critical, 5 high, 8 medium, 12 low)
   > "We've identified and prioritized all risks. The critical issues have remediation timelines and ownership. We're ahead of federal review cycles."

4. **Key Metrics Cards**
   - Budget utilization (78%)
   - Timeline status (On track)
   - Audit status (Clean)

**Investor Talking Point:**
> "This shows we have our house in order. Good compliance + clean audit history = lower risk for the grant program."

---

#### B. Frameworks Tab (2 minutes)
Scroll down to see all 8 frameworks.

**What to explain:**
> "We track 8 federal compliance frameworks. Let me walk you through what each one covers..."

Click on 2-3 frameworks to show:
- Compliance score (%)
- Risk level
- Number of critical issues and findings
- Detailed metrics

**Key Frameworks to Show:**

1. **2 CFR 200** (92% compliant)
   > "This is the main federal financial management requirement. We maintain documented cost accounting, competitive procurement, and annual independent audits."

2. **Section 504** (75% partial)
   > "This requires disability access. We have one critical issue here - website accessibility - which we're fixing in the next 60 days."

3. **NHPA** (90% compliant)
   > "This requires us to consult with tribes on historic preservation. Critical for TBCP. We have formal consultation procedures documented."

---

#### C. Risks Tab (2 minutes)
Click "Risks" tab to show issue management.

**Explain the critical issues:**

Issue #1: Website Accessibility
> "Our website doesn't meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. This violates disability access law. We're getting an accessibility audit and will have it fixed in 60 days. Clear ownership, clear timeline."

Issue #2: Asset Inventory
> "We have equipment that isn't in our inventory system. We're doing a complete physical count by the end of March. This ensures our financial statements are accurate."

Issue #3: Depreciation System
> "Our asset depreciation isn't integrated with our financial reporting system. This risks overstating asset values. We're implementing automated depreciation tracking by March 31."

**Investor Takeaway:**
> "Every issue is tracked, prioritized, and has a remediation plan. This is how professional grant management works."

---

#### D. Report Tab (1 minute)
Click "Report" tab to show the PDF export capability.

**Point out:**
- Executive summary (how you'd present to board)
- Framework breakdown with compliance percentages
- Compliance breakdown table (visual of all 8 frameworks)

> "This becomes our quarterly compliance report that we share with our board and file with federal agencies."

---

### Screen 3: Assessment Questionnaire (7 minutes)
**Click: "Start Assessment" tab**

#### Step 1: Framework Selection (1 minute)
Show the 8 framework cards with question counts.

> "Now let me show you how we actually assess compliance. We have structured questionnaires for each framework. Let me start with '2 CFR 200' - Financial Management."

**Click: "Start Assessment"** for 2 CFR 200

---

#### Step 2: First Question (3 minutes)
Show the first question from 2 CFR 200:

**Question:** "Does your organization maintain a cost accounting system that allocates indirect costs to federal awards?"

**Walk through the elements:**

1. **Question Text** - Clear, specific question
2. **Risk Badge** - Shows "CRITICAL" (red)
3. **Compliance Guidance**
   > "This explains what federal law requires - organizations must have documented cost allocation records."
4. **Investor Note**
   > "This highlights why it matters to investors - lack of proper cost accounting is the #1 finding in federal audits."

**Answer the question:**
- Click "Yes" radio button
- Add a note: "We use our accounting system ABC123 with cost center allocations"
- Click "Next Question"

---

#### Step 3: Second Question (2 minutes)
**Question:** "Are all procurement transactions for TBCP project items conducted competitively or with documented justification?"

**Show the different answer format:**
- Multiple choice instead of yes/no
- Still has guidance and investor notes
- Still has optional notes field

**Answer and proceed**

---

#### Step 4: Third Question (2 minutes)
**Question:** "Does your organization conduct annual independent audits that are submitted within 30 days of completion?"

**Highlight:**
> "This question directly ties to our 'Clean' audit status shown in the dashboard. Our processes ensure we pass audit."

**Progress visualization:**
- Show the progress bar at top (33% complete)
- Note "Question 3 of 8"

---

### Wrap-up (1 minute)

**Final Talking Point:**
> "This is how we systematically assess compliance across all 8 federal frameworks. Every quarter we complete this assessment, track responses, and generate a report. It's comprehensive, documented, and audit-ready."

## Questions Investors Ask During Demo

### Q: "What if you don't fix these critical issues?"
**Answer:**
> "That's why we track them proactively. If critical compliance issues aren't fixed, the federal agency can disallow costs (we lose money), suspend the grant, or even terminate it. Our process ensures we fix issues before auditors find them. Our clean audit history shows this approach works."

---

### Q: "How does this compare to other broadband organizations?"
**Answer:**
> "Most tribal broadband organizations operate ad-hoc without this level of systematic compliance management. We have documented controls across 8 federal frameworks. That's enterprise-grade compliance management. It makes us a safer grant recipient."

---

### Q: "What's the cost of this system?"
**Answer:**
> "This is built on modern technology (Supabase, Next.js) that scales. The real cost is the compliance officer time to maintain it. We amortize that across our $5M grant. As we grow to larger grants, the per-dollar cost drops significantly."

---

### Q: "How often do you run assessments?"
**Answer:**
> "Quarterly minimum. Before any big federal review. And immediately if we identify new issues. The system is designed to be living/breathing - updated as conditions change."

---

### Q: "Who uses this system?"
**Answer:**
> "Primarily our compliance officer and grant manager. Finance team reviews the reports quarterly. Board sees annual compliance report. Federal auditors get access during audit periods."
