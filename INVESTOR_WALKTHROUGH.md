## 2. **Compliance Framework Assessment** - Deep Dive

The system tracks compliance across 8 federal compliance frameworks:

### Framework #1: 2 CFR Part 200 - Uniform Administrative Requirements
**Status**: COMPLIANT (92%)
**Risk**: LOW
**What It Means**: Organization properly manages federal grant funds with documented cost accounting, competitive procurement, and independent audits.
**Investor Significance**: Shows disciplined financial management and reduces audit risk.
**Examples of Controls**:
- Cost allocation system for tracking indirect costs
- Competitive bid process for purchases >$25,000
- Annual independent audit submitted timely
- 7-year document retention policy

---

### Framework #2: Title VI - Civil Rights Non-Discrimination
**Status**: COMPLIANT (88%)
**Risk**: LOW
**What It Means**: Broadband services are delivered without discrimination based on race, color, or national origin.
**Investor Significance**: Demonstrates commitment to serving all populations fairly; reduces civil rights risk.
**Examples of Controls**:
- Written non-discrimination policy
- Grievance procedures documented
- Language access services for limited English proficiency populations
- No civil rights complaints in 2 years

---

### Framework #3: Section 504 - Disability Access Rights
**Status**: PARTIAL COMPLIANCE (75%)
**Risk**: MEDIUM
**What It Means**: Services must be accessible to persons with disabilities.
**Investor Significance**: Expanding addressable market to include ~26% of adult population with disabilities.
**Critical Issues**:
1. ⚠️ Website does not meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards
   - **Impact**: Non-compliant with disability law
   - **Remediation**: Accessibility audit + fixes (60 days)
2. ⚠️ Limited English Proficiency (LEP) plan not documented
   - **Impact**: May violate Title VI language access requirements
   - **Remediation**: Develop LEP assessment and access plan (45 days)

---

### Framework #4: ADA Title II - Public Services Accessibility
**Status**: PARTIAL COMPLIANCE (80%)
**Risk**: MEDIUM
**What It Means**: All public-facing broadband services must meet ADA standards.
**Investor Significance**: Inclusive design = better market penetration.
**Controls**:
- Physical location accessibility
- Telecommunications relay services
- Staff training on disability services (annual)
- Alternative format materials available

---

### Framework #5: NEPA - Environmental Assessment
**Status**: COMPLIANT (95%)
**Risk**: LOW
**What It Means**: Infrastructure deployment evaluated for environmental impacts.
**Investor Significance**: Permits/environmental challenges won't delay projects.
**Controls**:
- Environmental Assessment completed for all tower installations
- Mitigation measures for wetlands/wildlife impacts
- Documentation maintained for federal review

---

### Framework #6: NHPA - Historic Preservation
**Status**: COMPLIANT (90%)
**Risk**: LOW
**What It Means**: Infrastructure respects historic properties and tribal cultural sites.
**Investor Significance**: CRITICAL - Tribal consultation is key success factor for TBCP grants.
**Controls**:
- Consultation with State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO)
- Engagement with Native American tribes on project sites
- Documentation of all consultations and responses

---

### Framework #7: OMB 0660-0047 - Budget Certification
**Status**: COMPLIANT (85%)
**Risk**: LOW
**What It Means**: Annual certification that budgeted funds match actual project costs.
**Investor Significance**: Budget vs. actuals variance <5% shows excellent planning.
**Controls**:
- Annual budget certification submitted on time
- <5% variance between budget and actual expenditures
- Cost tracking system integrated with financial reporting

---

### Framework #8: Property & Financial Management
**Status**: PARTIAL COMPLIANCE (72%)
**Risk**: HIGH
**What It Means**: All equipment and infrastructure properly inventoried and depreciated.
**Investor Significance**: #1 audit finding area; indicates control gaps.
**Critical Issues**:
1. ⚠️ Asset depreciation tracking system not integrated
   - **Impact**: Overstated asset values on financial statements
   - **Remediation**: Implement automated depreciation (45 days)
2. ⚠️ Incomplete asset inventory for deployed infrastructure
   - **Impact**: Cannot account for all equipment >$5,000
   - **Remediation**: Complete physical inventory count (30 days)

## 4. **Intelligent Questionnaire System** - How Compliance is Assessed

When you click "Start Assessment," the system walks through intelligent questionnaires for each CFR code:

### Example Question (2 CFR 200 - Financial Management)
```
Q: "Does your organization maintain a cost accounting system 
   that allocates indirect costs to federal awards?"

Risk Level: CRITICAL
Expected Answer: YES (with documentation)

Compliance Guidance: 
Organizations must have documented cost allocation records. 
This includes accounting for both direct and indirect costs 
allocated to TBCP grants.

For Investors: 
Critical for grant compliance. Lack of proper cost accounting 
is the #1 finding in federal audits.
```

### Question Types:
- **Yes/No** - Does control exist?
- **Multiple Choice** - Multiple-choice assessment
- **Text** - Detailed narrative response
- **Date** - Timeline tracking
- **File Upload** - Evidence submission (policies, procedures, documentation)
- **Rating** - Maturity level (1-5 scale)

Each question includes:
✓ Compliance guidance explaining the requirement
✓ Investor notes highlighting business impact
✓ Support for uploading evidence/documentation
